🟩 Minecraft

Minecraft is the best-selling video game of all time. Developed by Mojang Studios, players can explore, build, survive and create almost anything inside procedurally generated worlds.

🎮 Gameplay

Minecraft features Survival Mode, Creative Mode, Adventure Mode and Hardcore Mode. Players gather resources, craft tools, construct buildings, fight mobs and explore a nearly endless world.

⭐ Famous Features

  • Redstone engineering
  • Massive multiplayer servers
  • Village systems
  • Huge modding community
  • The Nether and The End dimensions

🧱 Roblox

Roblox is one of the largest gaming platforms in the world. It allows players to create and play experiences made by a community of developers.

🎮 Popular Experiences

  • DOORS
  • Blox Fruits
  • Brookhaven
  • Dress To Impress
  • Tower Of Hell

⚡ PokéRogue

PokéRogue is a fan-made browser Pokémon roguelike. Players attempt increasingly difficult runs while building stronger teams and unlocking permanent upgrades.

⭐ Features

  • Huge Pokémon roster
  • Permanent progression
  • Roguelike gameplay loop
  • Browser based
difficult bosses.

⚔ Classes

  • Melee
  • Ranged
  • Mage
  • Summoner

🧸 Five Nights At Freddy's

Five Nights at Freddy's is a horror game franchise created by Scott Cawthon. The series is known for its animatronics, mystery-driven storytelling and large collection of games, books and spin-offs.

🎮 Main Games

  • FNAF 1
  • FNAF 2
  • FNAF 3
  • FNAF 4
  • Security Breach
  • Secret of the Mimic
