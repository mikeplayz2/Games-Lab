🧸 Five Nights At Freddy's

In the late 1970s and 1980s, business partners William Afton and Henry Emily founded Fazbear Entertainment, launching restaurants like Fredbear’s Family Diner and Freddy Fazbear’s Pizza. A tragic family and business collapse unfolded when William’s son died in the 1983 "Bite of '83," and Henry’s young daughter Charlie was murdered outside the diner, becoming the soul inside the Puppet. William then embarked on a dark killing spree, murdering multiple children who possessed the classic animatronics (Freddy, Bonnie, Chica, Foxy, and Golden Freddy), alongside his daughter Elizabeth, whose soul was trapped inside Circus Baby. Over the decades, William experimented with "remnant" (a substance tied to souls and memories) to cheat death, eventually getting crushed inside a spring-lock suit to become Springtrap. His surviving eldest son, Michael Afton, spent his life trying to undo his father's horrors—surviving a robotic "scooping" incident and later helping Henry trap and burn down the remaining haunted machinery in a massive pizzeria fire. Though the human characters seemingly perished, William’s consciousness survived as digital malware called Glitchtrap, infecting a virtual reality experience, manipulating a new generation, and awakening an ancient artificial intelligence known as the Mimic beneath a futuristic commercial Megaplex
