🟩 Minecraft

Minecraft is a sandbox game developed by Mojang Studios. Players explore procedurally generated worlds made of blocks and can gather resources, craft tools, build structures and survive against hostile creatures.

🎮 Gameplay

Players can choose between Survival, Creative, Adventure and Hardcore modes. Survival requires managing hunger, resources and hostile mobs, while Creative provides unlimited access to blocks and items.

⭐ Key Features

  • Near infinite worlds
  • Building and engineering systems
  • Redstone automation
  • Massive modding scene
  • Cross-platform multiplayer

⚡ PokéRogue

PokéRogue is a browser-based Pokémon-inspired roguelike. Players attempt endless runs while building powerful teams and unlocking permanent progression.

🎮 Gameplay

Runs become progressively harder as opponents scale. Strategic team building and resource management are critical.

  • Endless progression
  • Huge roster

🧸 Five Nights at Freddy's

Five Nights at Freddy's is a horror franchise known for animatronic characters and survival-based gameplay.

👀 Gameplay

Players monitor security systems while managing limited resources to survive the night.

🚪 DOORS
